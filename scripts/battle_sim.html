Battle mechanic

Wind engagement policy

Wind is set before a turn starts, so an attacker sees it and decides whether to engage. This models what they do with that information — it does not change how wind itself works mid-battle.

Parameters

Base wind roll — P(downwind), before engagement policy filters it
Attacker % + Defender % must be ≤ 100 — remainder is crosswind.
Runs entirely client-side — nothing leaves your browser.
