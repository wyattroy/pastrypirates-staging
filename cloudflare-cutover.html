4. Move the nameservers to CloudflareYou · 10 min, then waiting

Cloudflare → Add a site → playpastrypirates.com → Free plan. It scans your existing records. Check these two are there before you continue:

TXT  @  google-site-verification=QWA4b7b-vWs2-Blrgwmv51O72IBMg3NBpFvf3OYz2Cc
TXT  @  v=spf1 -all

Then at Squarespace, replace the nameservers with the two Cloudflare gives you.

Why this is safer here than usual — measured, not assumed: your domain has no MX records, so there is no email to break. Those two TXT lines are the only things that need to survive. Propagation is usually under an hour.
Leave the A records pointing at GitHub Pages during this step. Cloudflare copies them across, so your live game keeps serving from Pages exactly as it does now. This step does not move the live game.
