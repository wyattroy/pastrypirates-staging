Privacy Policy

Pastry Pirates is my game, and this is me telling you plainly what it records.

Two things, and neither of them leaves a cookie on your device.

  • How the game is played. Anonymised move data — what happened on the board — so I can see where the game is confusing or unfair and fix it. Nothing beyond the name you type when you pick how to play.
  • Basic visit numbers, through Google Analytics: which page you landed on, where you came from, and roughly where in the world you are. It is set up in what Google calls cookieless mode, so nothing is stored on your device and nobody is asked to consent to anything — which matters to me because children play this game. The trade is that I cannot tell repeat visitors from new ones, and I am happy with that.

There are no ads, no advertising trackers, and nothing is sold to anyone. If you would rather not be counted at all, any browser's tracker blocking will stop it, and the game plays exactly the same.
